Custom voice

Pitch: how high the voice is (tsukuyomi speaks at about 380 Hz; a typical man at 100–130 Hz). Formants: how big the speaker sounds. De-hiss: softens the highest frequencies. After the first Generate, letting go of a slider updates the Custom voice (speed: everything).

Listen

The first Generate loads the voice (a few seconds; the page may pause).

The dB number is a cleanliness score (harmonics-to-noise ratio): higher = less noise in the voice. Only useful for comparing; trust your ears over it.
